Task Wave + Note Link integration

From notes to action with Task Wave

Task Wave + Note Link integration is designed to bridge the gap between meeting conversations and task execution.

Overview

Task Wave Integrations unify the apps your team already uses into one smooth workflow. Instead of switching between tabs and copying updates, data flows automatically: meetings create tasks, files attach to projects, and deadlines appear on calendars. The result is fewer interruptions, clearer accountability, and faster progress.

Key Integrations

Unify your favorite apps and tools into one seamless system. From calendars and chat to video calls and cloud storage, every integration keeps projects organized, tasks updated, and collaboration effortless.

  • Slack & Microsoft Teams – Get instant task notifications where your team communicates.
  • Google Calendar & Outlook – Sync deadlines, events, and reminders automatically.
  • Zoom & Google Meet – Schedule and join meetings directly from Taskify.
  • Dropbox, Google Drive, Notion – Attach, share, and manage files in one place.
Your Responsibilities (As a User)

Unify your favorite apps and tools into one seamless system. From calendars and chat to video calls and cloud storage, every integration keeps projects organized, tasks updated, and collaboration effortless.

  • Choose and enable integrations that fit your workflow
  • Manage access and permissions for connected apps
  • Keep integrations updated for the latest features
Why integrations matter

Integrations matter because they eliminate the inefficiencies of scattered tools and disconnected workflows. By allowing your apps to communicate seamlessly, every task, meeting, and file stays in sync. This reduces errors, saves time, and ensures your team focuses on meaningful work rather than repetitive updates.

Benefits

Task Wave integrations unify the tools your team depends on into one connected system. By syncing calendars, chats, video calls, and file storage, Taskify eliminates repetitive work and keeps everyone aligned. No more switching tabs or chasing updates information flows naturally, tasks update instantly feels effortless. The benefit is simple: more productivity with less complexity.
