Frequently asked Questions & Answer

Scalable agents, real-time reporting, and full campaign control.
How quickly can you onboard my campaign?

We can have your dedicated call center team operational summaries instantly, so you can review insights immediately after your customer interactions ends.

Can I share notes with my team?

Yes! Every client gets a live reporting dashboard with real-time KPIs, call volumes, CSAT scores, and agent performance metrics. Reports are automatically shared with your team daily, weekly, or on a custom schedule.

What dialing platforms and CRMs do you support?

Our agents work with all major dialing platforms including Five9, RingCentral, Aircall, and Twilio. We integrate with CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho — keeping your pipeline updated in real time without any manual data entry.

How do you ensure my client data stays secure?

All client data is protected with enterprise-grade encryption both in transit and at rest. We sign NDAs, maintain strict data handling policies, and your contact lists are never shared or used outside your campaign.

Can I scale my team up or down mid-campaign?

Absolutely. Our team scales up or down based on your campaign needs with as little as 48 hours notice. Whether you need 5 agents or 50, we adjust quickly to match your volume and budget — no long-term lock-in.
