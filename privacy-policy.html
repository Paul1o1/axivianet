Privacy policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2025
Introduction

Welcome to Elite Call Center, a premier BPO outsourcing partner designed to help businesses scale through expert customer engagement and sales solutions. By accessing or using our website and services, you agree to this Privacy Policy. This policy governs how we collect, use, and protect data related to our professional BPO services. Our goal is to provide a secure, reliable, and transparent environment for all clients.

Eligibility

To provide a seamless and professional experience, Elite Call Center collects certain types of information from clients. This includes:

Business Contact Information: This includes details such as your name, email address, company name, and job title. Collecting this information allows us to manage our partnership, communicate project updates, and provide relevant support. It also helps us tailor our BPO solutions to your specific industry needs.

Usage Data: We gather information about how you interact with our reporting dashboards and service portals. This data enables us to analyze patterns, improve our reporting tools, and enhance the overall client experience.

Communication Data: We collect details about call volumes, campaign performance metrics, and agent interactions to ensure high-quality service delivery and SLA compliance.

Device & Connection Data: Information such as your IP address and browser type is collected when you access our portals to ensure security, detect potential issues, and maintain service reliability.

How We Use Your Information

At Elite Call Center, the information we collect is used to provide, enhance, and personalize our BPO services while ensuring the security and reliability of our operations. Specifically, we use your data to manage client relations, optimize campaign performance, and deliver actionable business insights. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Data Security

At Elite Call Center, safeguarding your business information is one of our highest priorities. We implement industry-standard security measures designed to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. All client data, including campaign reports and contact details, is stored securely with robust access controls to ensure confidentiality.

Your Rights

At Elite Call Center, we respect your rights regarding your data and are committed to giving you control and transparency. You have the right to request access to the information we hold about you and request corrections to ensure your data is accurate and current.

Cookies & Tracking

At Elite Call Center, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze portal usage, and deliver more relevant content. These tools help us understand how clients interact with our website, allowing us to continually improve performance and functionality.

Contact Us

At Elite Call Center, we value every question, suggestion, and feedback from our clients. Whether you need support, want to provide feedback, or simply have a question about our BPO services, our team is ready to assist you promptly and professionally.
